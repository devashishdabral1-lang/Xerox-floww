Xerox Flow · Aakhri update: 18 November 2026 · Privacy Policy dekhein

Ye shartein Xerox Flow ke use par lagu hoti hain. Service use karne ka matlab hai ki aap inse sehmat ho.

1. Service kya hai

Xerox Flow ek software hai jisse print shop QR code se customers ke orders le sakti hai. Print karna, uski quality, rate aur customer se paisa lena dukaan aur customer ke beech ki baat hai. Xerox Flow us lain-dain ka hissa nahi hai.

2. Dukaandar (owners) ke liye

  • Account banate waqt sahi jaankari dein, aur apna password surakshit rakhein. Aapke account se hui har gatividhi ki zimmedari aapki hai.
  • Customers ki files ko sirf print karne ke liye use karein. Unhe copy, save, share ya kisi aur kaam mein na laayein.
  • Koi bhi gair-kanooni, doosre ke copyright ka ulanghan karne wala ya apmanjanak content print na karein.
  • Apne rates, kar (jaise GST, jahan lagu ho) aur kanooni zarooraton ki zimmedari aapki hai.

3. Plans aur payment

  • Naye account ko 14 din ka free trial milta hai.
  • Plans (jaise Starter, Pro) mein har mahine ke online orders ki limit hoti hai. Limit poori hone par us mahine naye online orders nahi liye ja sakte.
  • Subscription ka paisa UPI se diya jata hai, aur payment verify hone par subscription chalu kiya jata hai.
  • Subscription ka mahina shuru hone ke baad refund nahi hota. Agar service humari taraf se lagataar band rahe, to hum baaki din badha denge.
  • Prices aur limits badal sakti hain. Naya price aapke agle subscription par lagu hoga.

4. Service rokna

Trial ya subscription khatam hone par, payment na hone par, ya in shartein ka ulanghan hone par hum dukaan ko pause kar sakte hain. Pause hone par customers naye order nahi de sakte. Subscription dobara chalu hone par service wapas chalu ho jati hai.

5. Customers ke liye

  • Sirf wahi file bhejein jise print karwane ka aapko adhikar hai.
  • Dikhayi gayi price dukaan ki hai. Payment counter par dukaan ko hota hai. Dukaan order ko mana ya badal sakti hai.
  • Print ki quality aur dukaan ki sewa ke liye dukaan zimmedar hai.

6. Guarantee aur zimmedari

Service "jaisi hai waisi" di jati hai. Hum koshish karte hain ki wo hamesha chalu rahe, par bina rukavat ya bina galti ke chalne ki guarantee nahi de sakte (jaise internet ya doosri companiyon ki service mein dikkat). Kanoon jitni anumati deta hai, hum kisi bhi nuksan ke liye us mahine ki aapki subscription fees se zyada ke liye zimmedar nahi honge.

7. Privacy

Aapki jaankari kaise use hoti hai, ye Privacy Policy mein likha hai.
